Delete your Dotyk account

Last updated: 2026-10-19

You can delete your account and data at any time, directly in the app.

In the app

  1. Open Dotyk and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email, date of birth and profile photo.
  • Your couple's shared space — memories and photos, notes, moments, dates, plans, games, drawings and your pet. This also removes it for your partner.
  • Push notification tokens and cached AI answers.

Deletion happens immediately. Copies in backups are overwritten within a limited period.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Subscriptions. Cancel Plus in your App Store or Google Play account settings — deleting the account does not stop payments.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies and legal obligations.

No access to the app?

Email hello.dotyk@gmail.com from the address you use in Dotyk with the subject "Delete my Dotyk account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.
